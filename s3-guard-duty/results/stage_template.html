What this means

Replication is the largest step

It takes about 22 s even for a 10 MB file and 36 to 43 s for 150, 200 and 500 MB. It is two thirds or more of the total for every file except the 100 MB one.

The scan itself is short

Between 2 s and 13 s for most files, growing slowly with size. One 100 MB file took 43 s and the cause is unknown.

Moving big files takes longer

The Lambda step grows from 1 s at 10 MB to 9 s at 500 MB, so copying to approved is not instant for large files.

Corrects the earlier pages

The first summary said the GuardDuty scan was 78 to 87% of the total. That split was estimated, and the replication share was guessed at 3 s without being measured. These numbers are measured, and they show replication, not the scan, as the biggest part. The "0.3 s per MB" figure on the second page is also not supported.

Each step boundary was polled about every 0.2 s from the client, so values are accurate to about 1 s. The scan step includes the delay between the object arriving and GuardDuty tagging it, not only the scanner. One run per size, so differences of a few seconds between files are within normal variation. Skipping replication by uploading straight to the scan bucket would remove the largest step, at the cost of changing the design.
